\chapter{Conclusions} % LaTeX chapter title
\label{ch:conclusions}

The work presented in this thesis was motivated by the need to better
understand the complex multiscale dynamics of atmospheric blocking and
\glsentrylongpl{wcb} in particular with regard to their associated uncertainty
and predictability. As outlined in the introduction, both phenomena are far
from being fully understood and still pose major challenges for weather and
climate models. While it the vast range of interactions and processes involved
makes it seem unlikely that a grand unified theory that combines both of these
phenomena will be developed in the near future, scientific progress in the
recent years has clearly elucidated key aspects, with improved understanding
going along with advances in the representation and prediction. 

This thesis has contributed to this progress not by proposing improved methods
or models aimed at practical forecasting, but by developing and applying novel
conceptual and theoretical tools to better grasp the full range of scales
relevant.
