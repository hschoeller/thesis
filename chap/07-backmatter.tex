\chapter*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Acknowledgements}

I am thankful for all the support I have received that has enabled me to
complete this thesis. It is the culmination of three decades of continuous
learning facilitated by my teachers, colleagues, parents, relatives, and friends.
I am fortunate to live in a society that values education and scientific
research
and supports its members in pursuing these endeavors. My research has been
funded by the
%\gls{dfg}
Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) through Project A08 of the
%\gls{crc} 1114
Collaborative Research Center (CRC) 1114
\enquote{Scaling Cascades in Complex Systems}, so all my education and
research was, by extension, financed by the German taxpayers, to whom I
want to extend my appreciation.

\bigskip

Scientifically, my particular gratitude goes to my supervisor, Stephan Pfahl
for his guidance, approachability and for generally being a very nice human
being.
The interdisciplinary project A08 has frequently challenged me to step
out of my comfort zone and successfully navigating the turbulent waters
of mathematics would not have been possible were it not for Robin Chemnitz,
who has
proven to be an excellent scientist, patient teacher and witty colleague;
as well as P\'eter Koltai and Maximilian Engel, to whom certainly the same
applies. The CRC 1114 in general and its early career researchers in
particular have also
provided an enriching and supportive environment that has frequently been
a source of inspiration and motivation. This environment was made possible
by the efforts of the coordination team and I want to thank Jana Jerosch,
especially, for her empathetic help and uplifting spirit.

Not a meteorologist originally, my transition into the field of atmospheric
science was facilitated by the welcoming and supportive community at the
Institute for Meteorology (IfM) at the FU Berlin and the
Weather and Climate Processes (WeCliP) group in particular, though I am also
grateful for the foundations laid during the years of studying at
the TU Berlin and University of Michigan. In particular, I would like to
thank Henning Rust for enriching discussions on statistical and other
matters of life; Thomas for computational assistance; Kalpanah,
Michael, Flo, Lisa, George, Mona, Dillon, Svetlana, Patrick, Ingo and Angela
for making WeCliP as warmhearted and inspiring as it is; for enriching
conversations and many good laughs. The early career
researchers at the
IfM have also been a source of diversion and recreation and the
Fachschaftsinitiative (FSI) has made the IfM the family that it is by
making the difference between a workplace and a community. My work
has also relied on the
facility management and administrative staff provided by the FU Berlin,
whom I thank.

\bigskip

Finally, the pursuit of this thesis would not have been possible without the
unwavering support of my family and friends that make me the person that
I am. I dedicate this thesis to my parents that have never once in my life
doubted my abilities and have always been pillars of support and encouragement
emotionally, financially and in every other way; my brother who
knows me like no one else and continuously challenges me to broaden my horizons;
Benita for always lifting my mood, Nita-Omi for countless joyful moments
and Lilo for her unconditional love.

I wish to thank Tina for accompanying me through defining years and for never
losing
her faith and love. I wish to thank Toni for a year
of care and passion that changed my life. Finding my path through good times
and bad would not have been possible were it not for the empathy, feedback
and solace of my friends---without a doubt, I love you all. I would like to
thank Lennart for knowing me better than I know myself;
Martin for his care and humor; Christoph for his encouragement; Max for
his advice in matters of life and science; Felix, Julius, Merle, Ivan, Feli,
Jacopo, Clara, Luise, Lea and so many others whose company
I cherish. Knowing you is a privilege and without you all, I'd be lost.
